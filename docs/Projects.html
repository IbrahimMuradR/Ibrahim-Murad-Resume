Delivery Management Tool

This is an interactive Delivery Management simulation tool that links Strategic Objectives to Programs and Projects. The content is cached in your browser but it can be exported in pdf format. It demonstrates my understanding and ability to manage cost, schedule, quality, benefits, risks, and change across complex, multi‑project environments.

The Strategy Roadmap provides a structured view of strategic objectives across management domains, delivery statuses and planned timelines. It allows objectives to be created, positioned and connected through dependencies, making it easier to see how strategic priorities relate to one another, when they are expected to be delivered and how progress supports the wider direction of the organisation.

The Program Management section turns strategic objectives into coordinated programmes of work. Programmes can be linked directly to the strategic objectives they support, bringing related projects together while providing programme-level views of milestones, schedules, costs, risks, issues, dependencies, change requests and benefits. This creates a clear line of sight between strategic intent and the coordinated delivery activity required to achieve it.

The Project Management section supports detailed delivery planning and control within each programme. Projects can be linked to their parent programme and managed through resource and work breakdown structures, milestones, schedules, costs, RAID information and change requests. The connection between projects, programmes and strategic objectives helps show how individual work products and delivery decisions contribute to programme outcomes and, ultimately, to the strategy.

You are invited to access the tool and explore it for yourself. Create a few objectives, programmes and projects, connect them together, and play around with the different views to see how the journey from strategy to delivery works in practice. And if you have any feedback please let me know :)
